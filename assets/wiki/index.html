    About this wiki

    crystal wrote this page about the repository from its code, with Claude.

    Reading it

    • The outline on the left follows you down the page; click an entry to go there.
    • Click a name in code to open the file at that line in your editor; ⌘ or Ctrl and click opens it on the forge, at the commit the page was made from.
    • The magnifier on a diagram opens it large: drag to move it, ⌘ or Ctrl and scroll (or pinch) to zoom.
    • Share copies a link to the section you're reading; the link beside each heading copies its own.
    • Chat answers questions about the code, reading it as it goes, with the section in view as context.

    Keeping it fresh

    crystal wiki update brings the page up to date with the code; crystal wiki serve shows it, with the chat; crystal wiki export writes it out as a static site.
